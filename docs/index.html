About verification

Every post here is signed by the publication's own key. A reader that understands Wirepost checks those signatures, so it can tell you whether a post really came from this publication and whether anything has been altered since.

That proves origin, not truth. A signature says who wrote something. It says nothing about whether they were right.

Once you have followed this feed, your reader remembers where it got to, and will tell you if the history it is offered later contradicts what it already has. The first time you subscribe there is nothing to compare against yet.
